# Dynasty

Live site: https://dynasty.hotelup.work

## About
Dynasty is a hotel, restaurant and events complex in Kamianets-Podilskyi, Ukraine, located at провулок Гімназійний, 4. The hotel sits directly above the restaurant, which serves Ukrainian, European and Japanese cuisine (including sushi and pizza) along with a wine selection and occasional live music. The site positions Dynasty as a place for short stays, dinners, family visits, business meetings and celebrations, with a banquet hall, a conference space and a children's room on site.

## Amenities
- Restaurant with Ukrainian, European and Japanese menus (sushi, pizza)
- Food delivery and takeaway
- Wine list
- Live music (schedule and terms to be confirmed before visiting)
- Banquet hall for celebrations, weddings, and corporate events
- Conference room for meetings, training and presentations
- Children's room for family dining
- Hotel rooms located above the restaurant
- Wi-Fi and air conditioning in the hotel
- Menu available online via a live ordering site (dynasty.choiceqr.com)
